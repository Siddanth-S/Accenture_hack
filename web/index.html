(01) — The blind spot

A stateless checker asks “is this response bad?” The failures that matter aren’t in a response at all.

They live in the trajectory. A fabricated fee asserted at turn two, reasoned from at turn six. The same question re-asked four ways, quietly quadrupling the bill.

None of it exists inside a single message. A single-response guardrail can’t see it in principle — not just in practice. Preflight accumulates risk across the session.
